01 How to use this guide

Who: All testers
Open: Your organisation’s test portal
  1. Ask the test coordinator for the correct portal link and your own test account. Confirm whether payments use the bank’s UAT environment before making a test payment.
  2. Find the chapter for your role. Each procedure gives the menu/page, numbered actions, the expected result, and a suggested test.
  3. Use a unique company CR and tender reference for each test run. Keep the same reference when handing work to the next role.
  4. Record results in the supplied TEST_CHECKLIST.csv. Report a failure with the page, reference, time, exact message and a screenshot.
Expected result

The team can follow one complete vendor-to-tender test and identify the step where a problem occurs.

Remember: Edition 1 • 5 October 2026 • English. This guide follows the current local application. Screenshots use sample accounts and records in an isolated test copy. They illustrate screens and states; they do not prove a real payment, SMS or email was delivered. Your live company names, fees, document types, permissions and dates may differ. Create and Edit Tender use the same six-step layout. No application changes are part of this guide.

02 Who does what

Who: Administrator and test coordinator
Open: Users, Vendor Portal and Tender role inboxes
  1. Prepare separate accounts for the administrator, procurement user, procurement manager, department requester, department manager, finance reviewer, technical evaluator and commercial evaluator.
  2. Prepare three different committee users: Chairman, Secretary and Member. Assign them to the tender; one shared account cannot replace all three confirmations.
  3. Prepare at least two vendor companies: one successful bidder and one unsuccessful bidder. Add a third invited vendor who does not bid to check recipient boundaries.
  4. Give each person the relevant role permission and company/department assignment. Menu visibility follows those permissions and assignments.
Expected result

Every handover goes to the correct person and company.

03 Sign in, OTP and choose a CR

Who: Staff and vendor contacts
Open: Sign in
  1. Enter your registered email and your own password. A vendor can enter the registered CR instead of email; the password must belong to an authorised user of that CR.
  2. Click Sign in. If OTP is required for your account, enter the current code delivered to your configured SMS or email destination and confirm it.
  3. If your email is linked to several vendor CRs, choose the company/CR you want to work with and continue. With one eligible CR, the portal selects it directly.
  4. Check the company name and CR shown at the top of the Vendor Workspace. Use Switch CR to change company when you have several memberships.
  5. Use Forgot password? if needed. If the OTP destination is missing or incorrect, ask the administrator to correct your account details.
Expected result

The correct staff workspace or vendor CR opens only after the required authentication.

Remember: Login OTP and the tender’s three opening codes are different. Never use a committee opening code as a sign-in OTP. A CR identifies a company; it does not grant access to a person who is not linked to that company.

Team test: Try email and CR login; test a shared email with two CRs; confirm another vendor’s CR cannot be opened.

04 Switch between your vendor companies

Who: Vendor with multiple authorised CRs
Open: Vendor Portal → Switch CR
  1. Click Switch CR near the company/CR badge.
  2. Read each company name and CR before selecting a radio option.
  3. Continue and check the badge again before adding documents, answering a tender or making a payment.
  4. Repeat with your second CR. Each company keeps its own profile, fee history and tender invitations.
Expected result

All actions apply to the selected CR.

Team test: Invite one CR to a closed tender; switch to another CR and confirm that invitation does not transfer.

05 Create staff users and assign tender responsibilities

Who: Administrator
Open: Tender Users master pages / Team and Roles & Permissions
  1. Open the relevant Tender Users page: Department, Department Manager, Finance, Committee, Procurement, Procurement Manager, Technical or Commercial Users.
  2. Add the person’s name and registered email. Enter the initial password when creating a new login and the correct phone/OTP details where available.
  3. Choose the correct company; add the department for department-based roles. Keep the assignment Active.
  4. Save. Confirm the user appears in the relevant role master and can see the expected inbox when signing in.
  5. When editing other details, leave password fields empty to retain the existing password. Fill them only when intentionally changing it.
Expected result

Assigned active users are available in tender team selectors and their own inboxes.

Remember: A general staff account alone does not guarantee inclusion in a tender team. The correct role-master assignment and the required permissions must also exist. The account’s configured OTP delivery method applies at sign-in.

Team test: Create a technical user in the correct company; confirm selection is available. Check a user assigned to a different company is excluded.

Administrator setup

06 Set vendor groups and registration fees

Who: Administrator
Open: Vendors Master → Vendor Groups
  1. Click Add or edit an existing vendor group.
  2. Enter Name and Code, then the Default validity days for the registration period.
  3. Set Registration fee in the displayed currency. A positive amount requires payment. A zero fee follows the waiver review process.
  4. For waived groups, ensure Riyadha is configured and applicable. A waived registration requires a Riyadha certificate even if the general checkbox is not selected.
  5. Keep the group Active and save. Check the fee summary shown when an applicant selects that group.
Expected result

Applicants see the fee and document rules for the selected group.

Remember: The OMR amounts in screenshots are demonstration amounts. Use the organisation’s authorised rates. Do not assume changing a group price rewrites an existing paid transaction.

Team test: Check one paid group and one zero-fee group. Confirm the zero-fee applicant is pending review, not automatically registered.

Administrator setup

07 Maintain effective vendor fee records

Who: Administrator
Open: Vendors Master → Vendor Group Fees
  1. Open the fee list and choose Add or edit the relevant group’s fee record.
  2. Select the vendor group and Fee Type: Registration Fee or Renewal Fee. Enter the amount/currency and Active From / Active To dates shown by the form.
  3. Make the fee record active for the intended period, then save.
  4. Select that group in a sample registration and confirm the application’s displayed quote before payment.
Expected result

Registration/renewal uses the applicable configured fee record and displays the payable amount.

Remember: Coordinate changes to active fee periods with the administrator. The applicant should pay the server’s displayed quote; entering an amount in the browser does not set the fee.

Administrator setup

08 Set group-specific registration documents

Who: Administrator
Open: Vendors Master → Vendor Document Types
  1. Click Add document type. Choose a vendor group, or the All vendor groups option when the type applies globally.
  2. Enter a clear Name and Code. Select Required for a mandatory upload and Active for a type currently in use.
  3. Save and repeat for each required type. Configure Riyadha Certificate for the applicable waived group.
  4. Open a sample registration, select the group and review the Documents section. Its mandatory types should appear as fixed upload rows.
  5. Change groups and check the refreshed requirements. Additional applicable documents remain optional unless the type is marked Required.
Expected result

Required uploads follow the selected group and cannot be relabelled by the applicant.

Team test: Configure two required types for one group. Try submitting with one missing, then upload both. Check a different group gets its own types.

09 Maintain grades, categories and specialties

Who: Administrator
Open: Vendors Master → Vendor Grades; vendor categories/subcategories
  1. Add/edit grade names, codes, ordering and active status in Vendor Grades.
  2. Maintain categories and subcategories used to describe supplier capabilities. Link each subcategory to its correct category.
  3. Review vendors’ submitted specialties before using them to target closed tenders.
  4. Use the Specialties Filter page to search vendors by their category/subcategory and related filters.
Expected result

Procurement can use clear, maintained grades and specialty filters when selecting vendors.

Remember: Creating a grade or specialty definition does not automatically assign it to every vendor. Check each vendor’s approved details.

10 Open the registration page and enter company details

Who: New vendor applicant
Open: Vendor Registration Application / guest_vendor
  1. Open the registration link supplied by the organisation. The sign-in page’s Vendor Registration button is currently hidden, so use the approved direct registration link.
  2. Choose the correct Vendor Group and read its registration fee summary.
  3. Enter the legal Vendor Name, company email and unique CR Number. Enter the telephone country code and number in the fields shown.
  4. Check VAT and other company details if applicable. Complete every field marked with an asterisk.
  5. Continue to Location, Account access and Documents using the section links.
Expected result

The application identifies the company, its CR and the registration rules to apply.

Remember: The company email and the person’s account email serve different purposes. Check both. If an existing login email is reused, use that account’s existing password rather than creating a conflicting login.

Team test: Try a duplicate active CR and a new CR. Confirm the application rejects the duplicate with a clear message.

The saved location belongs to the selected country and region.

Vendor registration

12 Create or reuse the applicant’s login

Who: Vendor applicant
Open: Registration → Account access
  1. Enter the applicant’s name and personal login email in Account access.
  2. Enter the applicant’s mobile number accurately, including the required country code. This is the person’s authentication destination when SMS OTP applies.
  3. For a new login, create a password and confirm it. For an existing email, enter its current password to link another CR to that identity.
  4. Check the applicant details before submitting; this person becomes the owner/contact for the new vendor CR.
Expected result

The applicant has one verified login identity with a membership for this CR.

Remember: Do not use another person’s email or phone. An inactive or disabled existing account requires administrator action; public registration does not reactivate it automatically.

Vendor registration

13 Upload the required registration documents

Who: Vendor applicant
Open: Registration → Documents
  1. Read the fee summary and the fixed required document rows generated for your selected group.
  2. Use Choose File for each required type. Upload a readable PDF or image in an allowed format.
  3. Enter issue and expiry dates where requested/applicable. Check that expiry is not earlier than issue.
  4. For a waived group, upload the Riyadha certificate as well as the other required documents.
  5. Use the additional document option for other applicable supporting files. Review requirements again if you change the group.
  6. Submit the registration and wait for the success/next-step message.
Expected result

Required documents are saved against their correct types on the vendor’s Documents tab.

Team test: Omit one required document and check submission is refused. Upload it and confirm the registration can continue.

Vendor registration

14 Pay a registration fee

Who: Applicant in a paid group / waiver applicant asked to pay
Open: Limited registration status page → Pay registration fee
  1. Sign in using the applicant’s email or CR and select the pending company if prompted.
  2. Read Registration payment required, the amount/currency and any Procurement message.
  3. Click Pay registration fee. Continue to Bank Muscat Smart Gateway and check the merchant amount before completing payment.
  4. Complete the bank’s authorised test/live payment and allow the browser to return to the portal.
  5. Check the application status and payment record. Registration completes after payment has been verified.
Expected result

A verified successful payment activates registration for the correct CR; its transaction is recorded for accounting.

Remember: A cancelled or failed bank payment leaves registration pending. If money was charged but the portal is still pending, ask accounting to check the existing transaction/reference before starting another payment. Screenshots do not show or certify the bank-hosted checkout.

Team test: Run an approved successful payment and an approved cancellation. Check cancellation does not activate the vendor and repeated return does not duplicate the payment.

Vendor registration

15 Wait for a waiver decision

Who: Applicant in a waived vendor group
Open: Limited registration status page
  1. Submit the required documents, including Riyadha.
  2. Sign in and check Pending waiver approval for the selected CR.
  3. Wait for the registration reviewer to approve the waiver or request payment.
  4. If payment is requested, read the reason and amount and follow the registration payment procedure.
Expected result

Profile, contacts and tenders stay restricted while waiver approval/payment is pending.

Team test: Confirm pending applicants can see status but cannot use full vendor features. Approve the waiver and confirm access becomes available.

16 Review Riyadha and approve a waiver or require payment

Who: Authorised vendor registration reviewer
Open: Vendors Master → Vendors → vendor details → Registration review
  1. Open the vendor and confirm its name, CR, group and pending registration status.
  2. Open View Riyadha certificate and inspect the uploaded evidence.
  3. If eligible, select Approve waiver and register vendor. Add an explanatory note if needed and save the registration decision.
  4. If not eligible, select Require payment. Enter a positive amount in the displayed currency and a clear Reason / note to applicant.
  5. Save registration decision. The applicant can see the requirement and pay; verified settlement then completes registration.
Expected result

The reviewer controls waiver approval or a specific payment requirement, with a saved reason.

Team test: Test the approval path and, using another applicant, the custom-payment path. Check zero/negative custom payment amounts cannot be accepted.

18 Add contacts and grant CR-specific portal access

Who: Vendor owner
Open: Vendor Portal → Contacts → Add
  1. Click Add and enter contact Name, Designation, Email and Mobile.
  2. Choose Portal access: Viewer for read-only work, Bidder for tender participation, or Editor for profile editing and tenders.
  3. For a new login, enter and confirm the initial password. For an email with an existing login, its existing password is retained across linked CRs.
  4. Mark the contact Active and Primary when applicable, then save.
  5. Ask the contact to sign in with their own email/password and confirm the intended CR and permissions.
Expected result

The contact is linked to this CR with the selected access, without overwriting an existing shared identity.

Remember: The free-text Role/Designation fields do not grant portal permissions. Use Portal access for that. Only an authorised owner can manage contact access; the contact form does not allow someone to assign themselves Owner.

Team test: Create Viewer, Bidder and Editor contacts. Confirm the viewer cannot edit or bid, and a linked multi-CR contact gets the CR selector.

19 Edit or remove a contact and manage passwords

Who: Vendor owner / authorised administrator
Open: Vendor Portal → Contacts; Change Password
  1. Use the contact’s edit action to correct details or adjust the permitted portal access.
  2. Leave password fields empty when only editing other details.
  3. Use the available password/access action only for the contact records that permit it. Shared-account password changes affect the login for all linked CRs.
  4. Deactivate/remove a contact only when access should end for that CR. Recheck that the person can no longer select that membership.
  5. To change your own password, open Change Password and enter the current/new details requested by the form.
Expected result

Contact details and CR access stay current; unrelated linked companies retain their own memberships.

Team test: Edit a phone/designation without re-entering a password. Remove one CR membership from a shared login and check its other valid CR remains accessible.

28 Block, unblock or remove vendor access

Who: Authorised administrator
Open: Vendors Master → Vendors → row actions

Tender requests and creation

30 Understand the two starting routes

Who: Department requester / procurement
Open: Tender Requests or Tender Procurement Inbox
  1. Use the Department Request route when a department initiates the requirement: Requester → Department Manager → Finance → Committee request approval → Procurement tender creation.
  2. Use New Tender in Procurement Inbox when procurement starts a tender directly.
  3. Both routes still require the tender’s Procurement Manager approval before publication.
  4. Read Request Source, Tender Status, Manager Approval and Workflow Stage separately in the inbox/register.
Expected result

The team knows which approvals belong to the request and which belong to the tender.

Remember: Request Source “Direct” identifies how the tender started. It does not mean manager approval was skipped. A department request’s “Manager approved” status is different from the tender’s Procurement Manager decision.

Tender requests and creation

31 Create a department tender request

Who: Department requester
Open: Tender → Tender Requests → Add
  1. Enter the reference, subject, description, request date, budget and other required request details.
  2. Check the company/department and configured Department Manager.
  3. Choose the tender type and evaluation method/weights. Select the proposed technical and commercial users and three different committee people.
  4. Add suggested vendors and other applicable details shown by the form.
  5. Save the draft. Use the Submit action on its row when ready; saving alone does not submit the request.
Expected result

The request becomes Submitted and is available to its assigned Department Manager.

Team test: Save a draft, confirm it is not in the manager’s actionable queue, then Submit and confirm the handover.

32 Approve or reject the department request

Who: Assigned Department Manager
Open: Tender → Department Manager Inbox
  1. Open the submitted request and review the requirement, budget, teams and suggested vendors.
  2. Approve if it is ready. Use Reject with a clear comment if it needs correction.
  3. Confirm the decision. An approved request becomes Manager approved for Finance verification.
  4. For rejection, the requester should read the reason, edit the request and submit it again.
Expected result

Only a submitted request is decided by its authorised Department Manager.

33 Verify the request in Finance

Open: Tender → Finance Inbox
  1. Open the Department Manager approved request.

  3. Use the Finance verification/approve action to continue, or Reject with a reason.
  4. Check that an approved request becomes Finance verified and reaches Committee request review.

Finance records a verification decision before committee request approval.

Tender requests and creation

34 Approve the request in Committee

Who: Authorised Committee request reviewer
Open: Tender → Committee Inbox
  1. Open the Finance verified request.
  2. Review the requirement, proposed teams/vendors and previous approvals.
  3. Approve it, or reject it with a reason for correction.
  4. Check that approved requests become Committee approved and are available to procurement for tender preparation.

The approved department request can be converted into a procurement tender.

Remember: Committee approval of a department request is separate from the later three-key Bid Opening process.

Tender requests and creation

35 Start the tender from a request or directly

Who: Procurement user
Open: Tender → Procurement Inbox → New Tender / approved request creation action
  1. For a direct tender, click New Tender.
  2. For an approved department request, use its available create/convert tender action; check the Linked request reference on the tender form.
  3. Complete all six setup steps: Tender Details, Milestones, Team Assignment, Target Vendors, Documents, Preview.
  4. Use Next/Previous or the step tabs to review the entire form before saving.
Expected result

A procurement draft contains the correct source and complete tender setup.

Tender requests and creation

36 Step 1 — tender details, method and fees

Who: Procurement user
Open: Create/Edit Tender → Tender Details
  1. Enter a unique Reference and a clear Title. Select Company and Department.
  2. Choose Open for the open invitation route or Closed for a targeted vendor audience.
  3. Choose the displayed Evaluation Method. Set Technical Weight and Commercial Weight; together they must equal 100%.
  4. Enter Tender Fees in OMR. Zero means no tender fee; a positive amount creates the vendor fee requirement.
  5. Write a clear Brief Description. Leave Submit for Procurement Manager Approval selected when ready for review.
Expected result

The tender’s identity, audience type, evaluation weighting and fee are explicit.

Remember: Open means eligible portal vendors can see the published tender. It does not mean draft tenders or sealed bids are public, or that an unauthorised login can submit.

Tender requests and creation

37 Step 2 — set all tender dates

Who: Procurement user
Open: Create/Edit Tender → Milestones
  1. Enter the Tender Release date/time and Last Date of Document Purchase.
  2. Enter Site Visit date/time when applicable, its Location, Mandatory attendance setting and Site Visit Instructions.
  3. Set Clarification Submission Deadline and Tender Submission Deadline.
